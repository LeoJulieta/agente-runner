Frequently Asked Questions

Here are some frequently asked questions about cards in football, along with their answers: 1. Q: What is the difference between a yellow and a red card? A: A yellow card is a warning given to a player for a minor infringement, while a red card is a more severe penalty that results in the player's expulsion from the game. 2. Q: Can a player receive more than one yellow card in a match? A: Yes, a player can receive multiple yellow cards in a match, but if they receive two yellow cards, they will automatically receive a red card and be expelled from the game. 3. Q: Do cards carry over from one match to another? A: In most cases, cards do not carry over from one match to another, but in some competitions, such as the World Cup, yellow cards can accumulate and result in a suspension for the next match if a player receives two yellow cards.
